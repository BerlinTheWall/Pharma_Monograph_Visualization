ℹ How to Read

Heatmap (top): each cell = % of products in that drug class reporting the AE. Darker = more common.

Treemap (bottom): click a row label to drill into that class. Each medicine becomes a block; its AEs appear as colour-coded leaf tiles inside it.

Linked selection: clicking a class in the sidebar or the heatmap row label updates both views simultaneously.

Press Esc or click outside to clear.
